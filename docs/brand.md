# Brand and presentation

Status: established direction, confirmed by Adam on 5 November 2026.

Carry the existing website theme through every public-facing artifact: articles, CVs, downloadable resources, diagrams, presentations and any future email or social assets. Adapt the composition to the medium while preserving the visual identity.

## Visual identity

- Warm paper: `#f7f7f0`.
- Primary text: `#203c34`.
- Deep green: `#174d43`.
- Muted text: `#646f66`.
- Fine rules: `#d9ddd2`.
- Quiet tinted panels: `#e9ecdf`.
- DM Sans for body text and clear headings.
- Instrument Serif italic for selective headline emphasis.
- IBM Plex Mono for short section labels, figure captions and supporting metadata.
- The `ar.` monogram, simple architectural line work, restrained diagrams and numbered sections.
- Article illustrations must vary in composition to explain their subject. Keep the visual language consistent, but do not reuse one stack of boxes with different labels for every topic.
- Compact editorial spacing, readable text and subtle interactions. Avoid oversized empty areas.
- Use the established components and tokens in `src/styles/global.css` as the implementation reference. Embed the same fonts in PDF exports and retain their licenses.

The design should feel personal, precise and calm. Original project diagrams and content provide visual interest. Do not reproduce a reference site's layout or illustration style wholesale.

## Voice and professional positioning

- Principal Architect, with hands-on engineering and engineering leadership experience.
- Contract roles first, with a clear secondary route for consulting enquiries.
- Explain constraints, decisions and outcomes in ordinary language.
- Be specific about Adam's responsibility and credit the teams delivering the work.
- Take ownership of what matters, see work through, and leave teams able to operate and evolve their systems.
- Keep a measured tone. Avoid self-congratulation, heroic narratives and universal prescriptions.
- Use `riddick.dev` as the intended primary domain and `adam@riddick.dev` as the contact address. Domain/email service configuration is separate from content.

For articles, use the [working voice profile](../skills/adam-riddick-writing/references/voice.md) and [writing workflow](../skills/adam-riddick-writing/references/workflow.md). This profile is versioned and develops through Adam's edits; the current site copy is not independent evidence of his natural prose.
